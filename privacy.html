Color Quest: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Color Quest is a colour-sorting puzzle game published by Siddiqur (siddiqur.com). This policy explains what the game does with information.

Information we do not collect

You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not run our own servers that receive your data.

Game progress

Your progress (levels, coins, streaks, settings) is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is not sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Website version

The web version at this address shows no ads and does not use analytics or tracking cookies. Like any website, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.

Android app: advertising

The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and approximate location, as described in how Google uses data in AdMob apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice later under Settings, "Ad privacy choices", when that option is shown. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings.

Some ads are optional: you can choose to watch a rewarded video ad to earn in-game coins.

Children

Color Quest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please use the contact details on siddiqur.com.
